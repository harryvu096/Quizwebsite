How to play

Screw Rescue

Tap loose screws into the tray. A lock snaps shut when the tray holds a full set of that color. If the tray fills with a mix you cannot finish, undo.

Sort & Serve

Tap a plate, then the table of the same color. Wrong tables break your combo and shave time. Clear the order before the clock ends.

Arrow Escape

Tap a tile to turn its arrow. Bolty walks whichever way the tile he stands on is pointing. Build one road to the flag, then press GO.
