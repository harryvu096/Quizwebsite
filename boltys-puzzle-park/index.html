How the park works

Screw Rescue

Tap an enamel screw into the brass tray. A lock shuts when the tray holds a full set of that color. Undo if the tray jams.

Lantern Sort

Tap a glass lantern, then the stall of the same color. A wrong stall breaks the combo and costs time.

Arrow Garden

Tap a stone to turn its arrow. Bolty walks the way the tile under him points. Build one road, then Walk.
