How to play

  1. Every line is one arrow. It only leaves from the head.
  2. Tap it when the straight lane in front is empty.
  3. A blocked tap costs a heart. Hold to preview, drag off to cancel.
  4. Pinch, double-tap empty space, or the zoom button to look closer. Drag to move when zoomed.
